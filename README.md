
Phishing is a form of cybercrime case whereby attackers send emails pretending to be trustworthy inorder to trick people and attain sensitive information eg student credentials and bank details.
Below is a guide which will prevent students and staff from falling prone to this attack and protect themselves.

Signs of Phishing :
•Poor spelling grammar
•Suspicious email adresses
•Unusual link and attachments 

#Common types of phishing attacks include:
•Smishing,where it involves text messages.
•Spear Phishing,done to target a specific person or organization.
•Email phishing,common one which is mostly sent to staff and students through emails.

#How can you protect yourself:
•Report
•Use Multi factor Authenticator
•Update your softwares
•Do not click suspicious urgent links

#Importance of these notices is to help people to be aware and to keep themselves safe.So be aware and make the right choice.
